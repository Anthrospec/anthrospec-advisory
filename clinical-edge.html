// 02 — METHODOLOGY

From clinical frameworks to building code

Tools like WeHSA and CASPAR were designed to evaluate how people function in their environments. We translate those clinical lenses into ANSI-aligned construction language — clearances, coefficients, contrasts, and structural specs.

01

Clinical Lens

We assess each room the way a clinician would: how a person moves, sees, balances, and cognitively processes the space — including how those abilities may decline.

02

Code Translation

Findings are mapped to ANSI A117.1 and best-practice thresholds: DCOF values, LRV contrast ratios, egress widths, lighting levels, blocking loads.

03

Build Execution

You get specifications, not suggestions — joist schedules, finish schedules, clearance diagrams — ready for your crew and your inspector.
