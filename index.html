// METHODOLOGY

Evidence-Based Protocol

01 // HAZARD EXTRACTION

Environmental hazard extraction. We identify binary friction points, egress limitations, and accessibility barriers directly from your site photos using standardized spatial safety matrices.

02 // FUNCTIONAL-SPATIAL MAPPING

Person-Environment (P-E) Fit calculation. We matrix the end-user's specific anthropometric and mobility profile against the identified geometric barriers to calculate spatial risk severity.

03 // ARCHITECTURAL TRANSLATION

Spatial-to-Architectural translation. We resolve the friction by converting mobility requirements into precise ANSI A117.1 spatial geometries (e.g., 60-inch turn radii and automated egress clearances).

04 // MATERIAL SPECIFICATION

Universal Design specification. We provide the hardware, fixture, and smart-home integration recommendations necessary to achieve compliance invisibly, avoiding institutional aesthetics.
