// METHODOLOGY

Evidence-Based Protocol

01 // HAZARD EXTRACTION

Environmental hazard extraction. We identify binary friction points, egress limitations, and cognitive triggers directly from your site photos using standardized clinical safety matrices.

02 // FUNCTIONAL-SPATIAL MAPPING

Person-Environment (P-E) Fit calculation. We matrix the end-user's specific functional profile against the identified geometric barriers to calculate spatial risk severity.

03 // ARCHITECTURAL TRANSLATION

Clinical-to-Architectural translation. We resolve the friction by converting functional deficits into precise ANSI A117.1 structural codes (e.g., shear force blocking and turn radii).

04 // MATERIAL SPECIFICATION

Universal Design specification. We provide the material and fixture recommendations necessary to achieve compliance invisibly, avoiding institutional aesthetics.
